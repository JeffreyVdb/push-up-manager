A quieter list of sets
27 October 2026
  • The day’s sets no longer sit between rules. One line now separates them from the daily goal, without the doubled line above the first set or the stray one after the last.
Every set lands on its day
26 October 2026
  • Logging a set now lands on that day in the calendar: the day lifts, its total drops in, and in Plant a wash of water crosses the fill.
  • Plant’s calendar greens cover a wider range, so a 10-rep day and a 100-rep day no longer look alike, in light and dark mode.
  • Classic no longer clips the Monday and Sunday columns of the calendar.
Goal progress fills the calendar
26 October 2026
  • Each goal day now fills from the bottom as you log reps, and reaches the top when you hit the target. You can see how close every day came, in light and dark mode.
  • Day numbers sit in the top-left corner so they no longer crowd the goal check.
Clearer progress in dark mode
26 October 2026
  • The daily-goal progress bar has a clearer outline in dark mode, making the full target easier to see in both Plant and Classic.
Release notes at a glance
24 October 2026
  • About shows release notes immediately under What’s new, without an extra tap to expand them.
Find your settings faster
24 October 2026
  • Settings now opens a clear category list on phones. Tap Appearance, Training, Account & security, or About to open its own screen.
  • Use Back to return to the list. On desktop, a wider window keeps the categories in a sidebar beside the selected section.
Light and dark, your way
24 October 2026
  • Both Plant and Classic now have light and dark modes.
  • Appearance follows your device by default. Choose System, Light, or Dark in Settings, independently of your theme.
  • Clearer control borders and button text in every mode. Navigation labels are centered, with no blue tap flash.
  • Settings are grouped into Appearance, Training, Account & security, and About. Find What’s new at the end.
  • Destructive actions now ask for confirmation in a desktop dialog or a mobile bottom sheet, including deleting sets and variations.
  • Read update notes here, even offline. A small indicator on Settings lets you know when there’s something new to read.
